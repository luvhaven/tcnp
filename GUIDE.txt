TCNP JOURNEY MANAGEMENT PLATFORM
Comprehensive Reference Guide & Demo Playbook

========================================================================
I. SYSTEM OVERVIEW & DEPLOYMENT REQUIREMENTS
========================================================================
TCNP Journey Management is a mission-critical logistics and location-sharing 
platform designed to rigorously coordinate protection details (Oscars) 
for high-value principals (Papas) during multi-day programs.

Expected Load Volume:
- Normally used 2 - 4 days a month.
- Peak usage: Up to 10 days in January.
- Active Field Officers: 10 to 50 concurrent personnel tracked via GPS.

MINIMUM CLOUD INFRASTRUCTURE (Vercel & Supabase)
Based on tracking 50 officers sending GPS fixes every 10 seconds, the app 
will generate ~1.7 million database updates and realtime websocket messages 
over a 4-day active period.

• SUPABASE (Database, Auth & Realtime engine):
  - Free Tier ALLOWS up to 2 million realtime updates per month. It might 
    suffice for the lighter months, but during the January 10-day peak, 
    it strictly requires the Pro Tier.
  - Recommended: Supabase Pro Plan ($25/mo). This grants 8GB database 
    storage, 5 million high-performance realtime messages, and removes 
    the project pause-after-inactivity rule. Highly necessary.

• VERCEL (Hosting & Next.js Edge Execution):
  - Free (Hobby) Tier works perfectly for immediate demos.
  - Recommended: Vercel Pro Plan ($20/mo). If this is deployed for 
    commercial organizational use, Vercel's Terms of Service requires a 
    Pro plan. It also provides drastically better Edge Function timeouts 
    which stabilizes heavy map loading.


========================================================================
II. ONBOARDING: SIGNING UP & THE SECURITY GATE
========================================================================
1. Public Sign-Up:
   - Officers visit the public '/signup' portal.
   - They enter their Full Name, Email, Password, and select their standard
     Team Call Sign (e.g. Tango Oscar, November Oscar). 
   - Note: "Sierra Oscar" has been completely stripped from this menu as 
     it is securely locked.

2. The Authentication Gate:
   - Upon successful signup, the user is NOT given access to the dashboard.
   - The platform strictly enforces a "Fail Closed" security posture.
   - New users are tagged as `activation_status: 'pending'` and are routed 
     to a locked "Awaiting Approval" screen.

3. Admin Approval (Command level):
   - Command/Admin logs into the dashboard and accesses `Manage Officers`.
   - Command toggles the new user from 'Pending' to 'Active'.
   - Once Activated, the Officer can successfully login and reach the hub.


========================================================================
III. USING THE APP & OSCAR ROLES
========================================================================
Every user on the platform belongs to a structural Role and an active Call Sign. 

1. Roles (The Hierarchy):
   • Command / Head of Command: Global visibility. Can approve officers, assign 
     roles, start/end journeys, and oversee the full operational map.
   • Admin / Dev Admin: Complete system access + structural control.
   • Tango / Alpha / November / Victor Oscars: Standard field protection teams 
     who log their arrival on site (Nests) and perform security checklists.
   • Delta Oscar: The highest-stakes field role. They stick exclusively to 
     the Principal (Papa) and manage the live transit procedures.

2. The Workspace (Dashboard):
   • The platform utilizes a Dark Mode ("Obsidian Luxury") aesthetic. 
   • In the sidebar, users navigate between:
     - Nests (AO security sweeps)
     - Eagles (Principal arrival checklists)
     - Operational Maps (Live GPS tracking)
     - Radio (Global Chat & Notifications)


========================================================================
IV. CORE WORKFLOW: HOW PAPA'S MOVEMENT IS MANAGED
========================================================================
The primary objective of the TCNP platform is securing the transit of 
the Principal ("Papa") from one location to another. 

The workflow is highly coordinated between the Delta Oscar (DO) and Command:

1. Initialization (Command / DO):
   - A new "Journey" is created from the dashboard.
   - Command assigns a specific Delta Oscar to a specific Papa. 
   - Command assigns the "Cheetah" (lead convoy vehicle).

2. Pre-Transit Sweep & Boarding (DO):
   - Before moving, DO executes the standard DO Checklist (e.g. verifying 
     Comms, scanning the Cheetah, organizing Tango Oscar formations).
   - DO updates the Journey Phase on the app to: 
     a. 'Moving to Eagle'
     b. 'Papa Boarded'

3. Live Tracking & SITREPs:
   - As soon as the Journey hits 'In Transit', the Live Tracking map becomes 
     the command center focal point.
   - The app actively pulls the DO's GPS coordinates every 10 seconds, 
     projecting them live across the Admins' map in real-time.
   - During transit, the DO is bound by the "15-Minute SITREP" rule. The 
     app runs a strict countdown timer. Every 15 mins, a push notification 
     forces the DO to submit a Situation Report to Command via the chat board.

4. Arrival & Hand-Off:
   - The DO updates the Journey Phase to 'Arriving at Destination'.
   - The DO triggers a "Hand-off to Tango Oscar", officially ending the 
     transit phase.
   - The journey is marked as Complete, and the location tracking gracefully 
     stands down.
